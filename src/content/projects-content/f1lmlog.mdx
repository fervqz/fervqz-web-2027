## Motivation

As a film photography enthusiast, I am always learning and correcting my technique. I kept wishing for a simple way to log shots with camera settings, lens choices, and lighting conditions to review later.

I tried notebooks, generic note-taking apps, and dedicated photography apps, but they were not designed for this workflow and quickly became cumbersome.

Instead of making film photography more enjoyable, logging felt like a chore that interrupted the creative flow.

That is why I built F1lmlog: a lightweight mobile app to quickly capture shot metadata and build a personal reference library for future learning.

## Motivation

The driving simulator was built as a custom mechatronics project combining embedded control, physical fabrication, and interaction design.

The objective was to deliver a robust, immersive setup that could be demonstrated reliably in public settings.

## Highlights

- Arduino-powered control pipeline
- Pneumatic actuation integrated with custom circuitry
- Rigid steel frame tailored for stability and maintenance

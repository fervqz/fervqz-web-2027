## Motivation

I really like coffee... a lot. I have been brewing with a V60 dryer for years, and always have the same problem: where do I put the brewer when I've just finished brewing my coffee and the filter isn't dry yet? A simple question with a not-so-simple answer.

## Inspiration

Some months ago I bought the <i>Industrial Design</i> book by Charlotte & Peter Fiell, and dived deep into the rabbit hole. At first I wanted to follow a brutalist design, but the 3D filament finish was not ideal for this kind of aesthetic. Knowing my limitations, I went for a modern-organic approach inspired by <i>Dyson</i> products.

This may sound like a lot of work just for a coffee dryer, but I knew this was going to be a product that I was going to use every day, so I wanted to make sure that I <span class="font-black underline decoration-2 decoration-wavy underline-offset-8 decoration-(--nav-link-active-color)">enjoyed the experience.</span>

## First-things-first

I looked at how I was handling the problem and how I could solve it right away. The first thing I used was another mug.
However, I didn't want to use an extra mug just for that; at least I had a starting point.

I designed a cylinder in Fusion 360 with the dimensions of the V60's base, problem solved, right? Not quite in an aesthetically pleasing way. From this first design, I iterated and tried a couple of variants until I came across the final version: an aesthetically pleasing, functional, and 3D-printable design that I'm really happy with.

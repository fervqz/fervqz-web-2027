## Motivation

Everybody can create a basic fitness app, and many people are doing it right now.

Most of my projects come from real-life problems. As someone who has struggled with following a healthy diet and maintaining a consistent routine, I wanted a more personalized way to stay on track.

FitBro is not just a macro tracker. It is a goal-oriented companion that uses AI feedback to help with consistency and decision making.

The product is designed to reduce friction, increase accountability, and provide a supportive path through the hard parts of fitness progress.

## Highlights

- Custom focus session presets
- Minimal interruption mode
- Weekly concentration trend cards

## Motivation

About a year ago, I found myself on a solo trip to Vienna, wandering through the city with no plan or itinerary. I stumbled upon a museum and park with free audio guides by scanning a QR code.

It barely scratched the surface of the rich history and culture around me, but it sparked a sense of wonder and curiosity that I had not felt in a long time.

Right after that, I asked an AI to generate a guide for the park I was walking in, then copied that text into a free text-to-speech app loaded with ads.

I was amazed by how much more connected I felt to my surroundings with a voice narrating stories and details of the place. It turned a simple walk into an immersive experience.

Inspired by that experience, I set out to create Sonoguides: a mobile app that brings those serendipitous discoveries to life with personalized audio guides for any location.

## Highlights

- Daily streak timeline with adaptive reminders
- Offline-first check-ins with instant sync
- Weekly insights panel with behavior clusters
